FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

Do credits expire?
Credits never expire. Buy a pack once and use them whenever you need. Free accounts receive 10 AI credits every week, forever.
What happens when APIs fail?
The Mobile Push Queue activates automatically. You receive a notification with your caption copied and media attached, then complete publishing through the platform's native share sheet. Failed publishes auto-refund credits within five seconds without requiring a request.
Which platforms are supported?
Full API auto-publishing: Instagram, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn, X. Quota-limited API: TikTok. Mobile Push Queue: Threads, Pinterest, Snapchat, WhatsApp Status, and any application with a share sheet. Every connected platform is publishable on day one.
Will AI reply without approval?
AI auto-sends only when confidence is at least 0.75, intent is simple (FAQ, compliment, lead, or spam), sentiment is neutral or positive, and conversation turn count is low. Refunds, complaints, legal threats, crisis language, and regulated industries always escalate to a human. The AI never auto-sends after escalation.
How does Nigerian pricing work?
Nigerian pricing is approximately 60–65% below global pricing through purchasing-power adjustment. Nigerian payments use Paystack (cards, bank transfer, USSD). Global payments use Stripe (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay). Credit packs start at $5 / ₦5,000.
Can I try TheWovenApp without signing up?
Yes. The interactive demo above lets you generate captions with 10 free demo credits — no account required. A free account gives you 10 more credits every week, forever.
