The problem

Social media runs on tools that don't talk to each other.

  • Creation lives in one tab, scheduling in another

    Caption in a doc, hashtags from a generator, media in a drive, then copy-paste into a scheduler. Every post crosses five tools before it ships.

  • Generic AI that doesn't know your brand

    Most AI writers produce interchangeable copy — the same tone for a law firm and a bakery — because they never learn who you are.

  • Conversations scattered across five apps

    Comments and DMs arrive where you published, not where you work. Responding means context-switching all day.

  • Analytics that report, but never inform

    You see what happened last month, but nothing connects it back to what you should create next.

The Woven way

One loom. Every thread.

Woven isn't another tool to add to the pile — it's a digital loom. Creation, planning, publishing, conversations, and insight run as one continuous workflow, and the AI carries your brand context through every step instead of starting from zero each time.

That means less time stitching tools together and more time making content that performs.

How it works

From blank page to published, in four moves.

  1. 1

    Set your loom

    Connect Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, and TikTok. Tell Woven your brand voice — tone, audience, what you never say.

  2. 2

    Create with context

    Generate captions, hooks, and hashtags in your voice. Upload an image and get a tuned hashtag mix, or describe a topic and start from a strong draft.

  3. 3

    Plan and publish

    Drag the draft onto your calendar, tailor it per platform, and schedule — or send it to every network in one click.

  4. 4

    Engage and learn

    Answer every comment and DM from the unified inbox, watch analytics roll in, and let the results sharpen next week's content.
